## Photo tips (before you generate)

1. **Face sharp and lit from the front** — soft window light beats harsh flash.
2. **One pet, uncluttered background** — the model has less to invent.
3. **Eyes open, natural expression** — avoid mid-blink frames.
4. **Crop to the subject** — leave a little headroom; do not crush the snout against the frame edge.
5. **Skip heavy filters** — feed a clean original.

## Prompt pattern (text or style notes)

Borrow the same structure used for other image/video models:

1. **Subject** — breed cues you care about ("ginger tabby with white chin blaze").
2. **Style** — oil portrait, soft pastel, studio photo, etc.
3. **Light** — "warm window light", "soft Rembrandt key".
4. **What must stay true** — "keep the exact blaze and eye colour".
5. **Negatives** — "no extra limbs, no text, no human face unless requested".

## Video clips

For image-to-video, describe **one** motion ("slow head tilt, soft blink") and lock the camera unless you want a move. Keep clips short while you learn the credit cost shown in the product UI.
